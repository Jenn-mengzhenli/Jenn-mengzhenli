# Mengzhen Li

Computer Science graduate from Xiamen University Tan Kah Kee College in China (June 2026).

My undergraduate research focused on control algorithms and simulation for a dual-quadrotor UAV system. I worked on a Fuzzy CPSO-BP neural-network PID controller and evaluated its performance in simulation. This work contributed to a paper presented at ICAIRC 2025.

## Research interests

- Advanced, robust, and adaptive control for aerial robots
- Moving from simulation to hardware experiments
- Learning more about multi-UAV coordination, state estimation, and motion planning
